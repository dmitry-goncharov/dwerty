"FileEnc" Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026
IMPORTANT: BY USING THIS MOBILE APPLICATION, YOU SIGNIFY YOUR AGREEMENT TO OUR PRIVACY POLICY. IF YOU DO NOT AGREE TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY, PLEASE DO NOT USE THIS MOBILE APPLICATION.

"FileEnc" ("Us" or "We" or "Our"), developed by Dwerty, is committed to respecting the privacy rights of the users of its mobile application, as well as its related services. We created this Privacy Policy to inform you and provide necessary disclosures regarding our collection and using of information when you use our mobile application. We collect and use some of your information, as described below, to provide our mobile application and to measure and improve it over time.

1. No Data Collection and Transmission

The Application operates completely offline. We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any Personal Data, sensitive user data, or device-identifying information. Since the Application does not connect to the internet, it is technically impossible for any data to be transferred from your device to our servers or any third parties.

2. Local Processing Only

Any files, text, or data processed, encrypted, or decrypted within the Application remain strictly on your device's internal storage. No local data is accessible to the developer or any external services.

3. No Analytics and Third-Party SDKs

The Application does not contain any third-party code, advertising networks, analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or crash reporting libraries that could track your activity or collect log data.

4. Device Permissions and All Files Access (File Management)

To perform its core functions as a file manager and local security tool, the Application requires the MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (All Files Access) permission. This permission is strictly necessary to allow users to locate, view, organize, move, delete, encrypt, and decrypt files and folders throughout the device's shared storage. Without this broad access, the Application cannot fulfill its primary purpose of core file management operations outside its isolated sandboxed directory. All file browsing and processing activities are executed entirely locally. No file content, directory structures, or metadata are ever collected, stored externally, or transmitted over the network.

5. Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any information from any users, including children, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect or process personal data from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. However, the core principle of total privacy and zero data collection will remain unchanged. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
