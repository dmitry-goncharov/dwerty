"OTXform" ("Us" or "We" or "Our"), developed by Dwerty, is committed to respecting the privacy rights of the users of its mobile application, as well as its related services. We created this Privacy Policy to inform you and provide necessary disclosures regarding our collection and using of information when you use our mobile application. We collect and use some of your information, as described below, to provide our mobile application and to measure and improve it over time.

1. Data Minimization and Ephemeral Processing

The Application provides collaborative note editing functionality. To identify who is actively making changes in a shared document, users can provide a temporary Name upon connecting. This name, along with the note content, is transmitted to our server for real-time synchronization and remains accessible via a unique Note ID for a short duration. We do not retain note text, names, logs, or history on our servers once the active session closes.

2. No Analytics and Third-Party SDKs

The Application strictly excludes all third-party trackers, analytical tools, advertising networks, or automated crash reporting libraries. Your document content, entered names, and application usage remain completely private.

3. Network Permissions and Transmission Security

Internet access is utilized exclusively to establish a connection with the server for note synchronization and collaboration features. While we strive to protect your data during transit, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under thirteen (13). Since note processing and user names are ephemeral and do not involve permanent data logging, no personal identifiers are permanently harvested or stored.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time, while strictly maintaining our core principles of zero permanent data logging and an ad-free user experience.
