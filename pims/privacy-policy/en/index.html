"Pims" Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026
IMPORTANT: BY USING THIS MOBILE APPLICATION, YOU SIGNIFY YOUR AGREEMENT TO OUR PRIVACY POLICY. IF YOU DO NOT AGREE TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY, PLEASE DO NOT USE THIS MOBILE APPLICATION.

"Pims" ("Us" or "We" or "Our"), developed by Dwerty, is committed to respecting the privacy rights of the users of its mobile application, as well as its related services. We created this Privacy Policy to inform you and provide necessary disclosures regarding our collection and using of information when you use our mobile application. We collect and use some of your information, as described below, to provide our mobile application and to measure and improve it over time.

1. Complete Offline Operation and Data Minimization

The Application is designed to operate strictly as an offline utility. No personal data, account registration, or configuration logging is required to use the service. All application state and configurations are stored exclusively on your local device and remain under your full control.

2. Shizuku Framework Implementation

The Application utilizes the Shizuku framework solely to execute its core local system functions via the Android Debug Bridge (ADB) privilege context. This local interface is used transparently and ethically:

  • The Application does not abuse Shizuku privileges to read, export, or log your personal data, private files, or third-party app information.
  • All operations performed via Shizuku are processed entirely on-device and are never transmitted outside the application.

3. No Analytics and Third-Party SDKs

The Application strictly excludes all forms of third-party trackers, analytics platforms, advertising networks, telemetry, or automated crash reporting tools. Your interactions with the application are entirely private and invisible to us and any third parties.

4. No Network Permissions

The Application does not declare or utilize network permissions. Because there is no access to the internet, it is technically impossible for the application to transmit data from your device to external servers or remote entities.

5. Children's Privacy

Since the Application operates fully offline and does not collect, log, or transmit any data whatsoever, we do not knowingly or technically possess the ability to gather personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or 16 in the EU).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. However, any future updates will strictly maintain our core principles of zero data collection, absolute user privacy, and offline operation.
